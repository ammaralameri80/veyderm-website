VEYDERM LOGO KIT
================

WHICH FILE TO USE
- veyderm-logo.svg ............ Main logo, on white / light backgrounds (website header, print)
- veyderm-logo-dark.svg ....... On dark or purple backgrounds (footer, dark slides)
- veyderm-logo-small.svg ...... Heavier strokes for small sizes (below ~28px tall, mobile, email signatures)
- veyderm-logo-small-dark.svg . Same, for dark backgrounds
- veyderm-logo-mono-black.svg . One-colour black (fax, stamps, single-colour print)
- veyderm-logo-mono-white.svg . One-colour white (photos, dark print)
- veyderm-icon.svg ............ Standalone "v" symbol on purple tile (app icon, mobile)
- veyderm-icon-square.svg ..... Full-bleed square tile (platforms that round/circle-crop themselves)
- veyderm-icon-transparent.svg  Purple "v" only, no background
- veyderm-icon-white.svg ...... White "v" only, for dark backgrounds
- favicon.svg + favicon-16/32/48.png ... Browser tab icon
- apple-touch-icon-180.png .... iPhone home-screen icon
- veyderm-icon-512.png ........ App stores / PWA
- social-avatar-1024.png ...... Instagram, LinkedIn, X, WhatsApp Business profile
- *-2000.png .................. Transparent PNGs for Word, PowerPoint, Canva

All SVGs and PNGs have transparent backgrounds (except the icon tiles).

COLOURS
- Brand purple  #6C65C2  (contrast on white 4.9:1 — passes WCAG AA)
- Ink black     #111111  (contrast on white 18.9:1 — passes WCAG AAA)
- Lavender      #B3AAF0  (for "vey" on dark purple — 5.4:1 on #362B87)
- Icon gradient #7A72D1 -> #4B40B0

RULES
- Clear space: keep empty space around the logo at least equal to the
  height of the lowercase letters (the "e" in "vey").
- Minimum size: full logo 80px wide on screen (use the -small version below
  ~28px tall); icon 16px.
- Don't stretch, recolour, add shadows, or place the colour logo on busy photos
  (use mono-white instead).
